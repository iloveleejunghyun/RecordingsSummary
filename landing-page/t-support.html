FAQ

How do I get started?

Open the app and tap Record. Speak normally, then tap Stop when you're done. We'll transcribe the recording and generate a summary automatically.

Is there a recording length limit?

During this beta, recordings are capped at 5 minutes so transcription stays fast and reliable. We're working on support for longer recordings.

Do transcription and AI summaries need an internet connection?

Yes. Both speech-to-text and AI summarization rely on online services, so please stay connected. If a service is temporarily unavailable, the app will clearly show a failure with a Retry option rather than substituting fake data.

Where is my recording data stored?

Your recordings, transcripts, and summaries are stored locally on your phone and are not uploaded to a server for long-term storage. Audio and transcript content is only sent temporarily to the relevant AI service when generating a transcription or summary.

Can I listen back to a recording?

Yes — open any recording's detail screen and tap "Play Recording" to hear the original audio.

A recording failed to transcribe or summarize — what now?

Open the recording and tap "Retry." If it keeps failing, check your connection, or email us with the approximate time it happened.

How do I delete a recording?

Open the recording's detail screen and tap "Delete Recording" at the bottom. Once confirmed, it's deleted along with its audio, transcript, and summary, and cannot be undone.
