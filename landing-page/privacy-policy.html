TalkSum Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-18

What Data We Collect

  • Recording content: The audio recordings, transcripts, and summaries you create are stored locally on your phone and are not uploaded to our servers for long-term storage.
  • Sent temporarily to AI services: When you record, your audio is sent to a third-party speech-to-text provider to generate a transcript, and that transcript is sent to a third-party AI provider to generate a summary — both in real time to produce results. We don't keep our own copy of this content.
  • Basic usage analytics: The app records a small amount of anonymous usage activity (e.g. whether a recording completed, whether transcription/summarization succeeded) to understand feature usage and improve the product — this does not include the actual audio, transcript, or summary content.

Third-Party Services

  • Volcengine (Doubao) Speech Recognition: used to convert your recordings into text.
  • Baidu Qianfan / ERNIE (百度智能云 千帆 / 文心一言): used to generate a summary from your transcript.
  • DCloud uni统计 (analytics): used for basic in-app usage analytics.

These providers handle the data they receive under their own privacy policies. We don't use your recording content for any purpose unrelated to these features, and we don't sell it to third parties.

About the "Tracking" Permission Prompt

The app may include a system-level tracking-permission prompt, which comes from a base component of the development framework we use. TalkSum itself does not use your data for cross-app / cross-website ad tracking, and does not share data with data brokers.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete any recording at any time from its detail screen. Once deleted, the audio, transcript, and summary are removed from your device and cannot be recovered. Uninstalling the app also clears all recording data stored on your device.
